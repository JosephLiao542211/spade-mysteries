LIGHTS OUT AT THE BLACK SPADE
Windows jam edition

Extract the entire Black Spade folder from the ZIP, then open Black Spade.exe.
Keep the files together. Allow at least 6 GB for the extracted game in addition
to the download. The game opens in a browser window and runs on this PC.
No account, Internet connection, Python installation or Unreal installation is
needed to play.

Read the briefing while the local dialogue model loads. Choose a witness, type
a question and wait for their response. Watch fear and the second meter and
check how the witness interpreted your line. When a witness makes a statement,
the notebook records it. Try repeating that statement to another witness.
Accusing a suspect ends the case. You can start a fresh case from the ending.

The Windows x64 build includes Gemma 4 E4B, the Vulkan inference backend and a
CPU backend. GPU inference requires a compatible Vulkan driver. On a CPU,
loading and replies can take longer. If startup fails, check the model logs in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Black Spade\logs. Closing the game ends the current session.

Voice also runs on this PC. The suspects speak their lines; turn that off with
Voices on/off at the top. To talk instead of typing, press Speak beside the
question box, allow the microphone when the window asks, and ask your question.
It is written into the box as you talk and stops when you do. Press Enter to
send it, or correct it first. Headphones keep the suspects' voices out of the
microphone.

Third-party credits and licenses are in the licenses folder.
